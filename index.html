Our School Objectives

At Phebeth-PEC International School, our objectives are focused on developing the whole child.

01

Quality Education

Provide quality education that develops the intellectual, social, emotional and practical abilities of every child.

02

Academic Excellence

Promote academic excellence by encouraging pupils to learn, explore, think critically and achieve their full potential.

03

Moral Character

Build strong moral character by teaching honesty, respect, discipline, responsibility, kindness and integrity.

04

Confident Learners

Develop confident and independent learners who can communicate effectively and make sound decisions.

05

Creativity & Innovation

Encourage creativity and innovation by providing opportunities for children to discover and develop their talents.

06

Leadership Development

Develop leadership skills and prepare pupils to become responsible and productive members of society.

07

Safe Environment

Create a safe and supportive learning environment where every child feels valued, respected and motivated to succeed.

08

Life & Digital Skills

Equip pupils with relevant life skills and digital skills needed to function effectively in a changing world.

09

Teamwork

Encourage teamwork and positive relationships among pupils, teachers, parents and the wider community.

10

A Successful Future

Prepare every child for a successful future by combining sound academic knowledge with good character, confidence and practical skills.
